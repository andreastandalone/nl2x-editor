# NL2X Editor 0.1.0-beta.1

The first public beta of NL2X Editor, a free, open-source editor for the Clavia Nord Lead 2X (and Nord Rack 2X) for macOS: a VST3 and Audio Unit plugin and a standalone app, as one Universal download for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

It is a **beta**: it has been used on one real Nord Lead 2X, in Ableton Live and Cubase, but not by many people yet. **Percussion Kits and Performances cannot be edited yet.** Back up your Banks before writing to the synth.

Download `NL2X-Editor-0.1.0-beta.1-macOS-universal.zip`, unzip it and follow `INSTALL.txt` or the [README](https://github.com/andreastandalone/nl2x-editor#installation). The build is not notarized: macOS asks you to allow it once (README, "Opening it the first time").

## What it does

- **Slot editing.** Every sound parameter of the Programs on Slots A–D, including the Velocity/Morph Amounts the synth's panel hides (Option-drag a knob, shown as a ring around it). Two-way: the synth's panel moves the editor's controls, and the editor's controls move the synth.
- **DAW integration.** Every parameter is a DAW parameter ("A: Filter Cutoff", "B: LFO1 Rate Morph"...), for automation and controller mapping. The project remembers the four Slots' sounds and sends them when it is opened (optional); Bank/Program selections and manual Slot Channel overrides are kept with it, and opening a project sends no Program Change.
- **Selectors.** Bank and Program for each Slot (Banks 0–9, Programs 1–99 and the Kits P0–P9), and the Performance selector.
- **Connection check.** Finds the synth's Global Channel by itself, checks both cables, SysEx and Control Change, and says what to change when something is wrong. Warns when a second editor controls the same synth (Take control).
- **Library.** Your `.syx` files in `~/Documents/Nord Lead 2X/` (or a folder you choose), each subfolder a group: single Programs, Kits, Performances and whole Banks. Click to audition on the selected Slot without storing. Names, any number of tags and favourites per sound in `library.json`, with filters, suggested tags and Auto-tag.
- **Librarian.** Reads the synth's RAM Program Banks 0–3 and shows them beside the Library. Drag Programs from the Library or from other locations into a Bank, store a Slot or a Library Program into a location, replace a Bank with a Bank file, and send the changed locations or the whole Bank. Every write backs the Bank up first to `~/Documents/Nord Lead 2X/Backups/`, asks before overwriting, switches the lit Slot to the target Bank and back (unsaved edits kept) or shows the panel steps when it cannot, and reads the written locations back.
- **Undo and redo** of edits, also with Cmd+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z.
- **Saving** a Slot, a Performance, a Library entry or a Bank as standard `.syx` files.
- **Window** resizable from 75 % to 200 %, with fixed sizes in the menu next to the name.
- **Standalone app** with the same editor, and a **Probe** window for hardware discovery (for developers).

## Known limitations

- **Percussion Kits:** no editing, and no Kit Banks in the Librarian. A Slot playing a Kit shows a notice. Kits in the Library can be auditioned, saved as `.syx` and sent from the Library. The synth's replies to Kit Bank reads change from read to read, so reading and writing Kit Banks is not understood yet (#33).
- **Performances:** the Performance view is read-only. The synth stores a Performance dump it receives but does not apply it, so editing Split, Layer or Slot Channels from the editor would not change what it plays (#31). Performance automation parameters are refused for the same reason. Writing Performance Banks is not supported (#34). Project recall restores the Slots' sounds, not the Performance settings.
- **After a Performance audition** (clicking a Performance in the Library), a Store asks you to switch the lit Slot to the target Bank by hand, because the synth then reports the sent Performance instead of what its panel shows.
- **Banks 4–9** are ROM on the synth: selectable, never written.
- **Writing** expects P and C on (Shift + PROG/CTRL), and the lit Slot on its own MIDI channel for the automatic Bank switch. Do not play or touch the synth during a write.
- **macOS only**, 11 or later. Not signed with a Developer ID and not notarized.
- **Hosts:** tested in Ableton Live and Cubase (VST3); the AU passes `auval`. Corner resizing and the size menu have not been checked by hand in every host. Some hosts keep Cmd+Z for their own undo.

## Still unverified on hardware

These rest on the manual or on our own inference and are listed in `engine/include/nl2x/HardwareAssumptions.h` and [docs/open-questions.md](../open-questions.md). Reports from your synth are welcome.

- **Morph Amount scale:** whether a Morph Amount moves its parameter in the units of the knob value, as the Morph ring draws it (#41).
- **Layered Slots** also receiving on the leftmost active Slot's channel (manual p.76–77).
- **The names of value 0** of the stepped buttons other than Filter Type and LFO2 Dest/Arp Mode (their order is verified, the names are inferred), and Filter Distortion as CC80 driving bit 4 of byte 52.
- **Knob CCs** mapping one to one onto the SysEx values, in both directions; reverse CC sweeps.
- **The "off" Slot Channel** stored as −1 in a Performance.
- **A kept Performance dump:** whether selecting a Program or Performance on the panel replaces a Performance the synth received.
- **Kit reads:** a read of a Slot holding a Kit is reliable only for the lit Slot (#33).
- **Bank 0 Programs 1–4 and 17** were found empty once during development, with no write from the editor that could explain it (#32). Compare your Banks with your backups after the first writes, and report anything unexpected.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue at <https://github.com/andreastandalone/nl2x-editor/issues> with the editor's version (About), macOS version, Mac (Intel or Apple Silicon), DAW and version (or the standalone app), plugin format, MIDI interface, the synth's settings (P, C, Global Channel, Slot Channels), what you did, what you expected and what happened. For a write, copy the task window's text into the issue. Please do not attach Clavia's factory Banks or manuals.
